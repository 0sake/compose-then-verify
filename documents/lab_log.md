30,038 (query, source) pairs of the mirror: skewness 10.37, 198 images (top-1%)

Illegitimate hubs -> green light according to the declared rule.

maximum**. No estimator can exceed +0.005.

**Verdict:** falsified. Hubness exists (skew 10.37) but is not the bottleneck:
with a dense GT, moderate hubs are legitimate targets and removing popularity

## Round 2, Idea 2 — Margin solve: Gram diagnosis (Jul 18, preliminary)

Declared predictions vs measured (paired directions, 12 benchmark attributes):
|cos|(Lipstick, Heavy_Makeup) predicted ≥ 0.6 → **0.491** (below the prediction,
above the 0.3 death threshold); |cos|(Male, Mustache) predicted high → **0.010,
FALSIFIED**: Hamming pairing has already removed the Male–Mustache correlation
(consistent with the P2 drift diagnosis — on class means it is 0.824). The
failure of -Male,-Mustache is NOT interference between directions.

**Interference is real but localized to ONE pair.** In the B3-paired edit
(α, β) = (0.75, 0.50) of the lipstick triple, the signed lip–makeup cross term
is −0.184: along d_makeup the intended push −0.50 becomes −0.132
(**74% cancelled** by +Lipstick); along d_lipstick +0.75 → +0.504 (33%).
The global cancellation ratio (0.941) hides it because Smiling is orthogonal:
the interference lives entirely in the lip/makeup plane. All other
co-occurring pairs: |cos| ≤ 0.175.

**Structural fact:** on class means (= probe directions)
cos(lip, makeup) = **0.993** — in CLIP the two statistical separations are the
same direction; the paired ones halve it (0.491). Consequence for the solve:
with probe normals the lipstick-triple system is nearly singular
(**κ = 318**); with paired normals κ ≤ 3.2 everywhere → the solve must be
formulated on the paired directions.

**Declared verdict: thesis alive** (not all co-occurring pairs ≤ 0.3) → proceed
with the minimum-norm solve. Expectation scaled down: interference explains
lipstick (0.000), not -Male,-Mustache; on the other queries the solve can only
act on dilution.

## Round 2, diagnosis overturned — Full-predicate re-rank (Jul 19, mirror only)

Re-reading the I1 autopsy: the 0.357 ceiling is the oracle of the constraint
verification ALONE (osat, +0.061); the ceiling of ANY re-ranking of the top-200
is the coverage **R@200cov = 0.840**. The 0.357→0.840 gap is the other half of
the relevance predicate — Hamming ≤ 2 from the source on the ~37 attributes NOT
edited — which no method tried so far had ever scored: B8/B9 and H4 (falsified)
were linear full-gallery sums over mean-diff probes, not an error budget over
candidates.

**Diagnostic method** ([`src/predicate_rerank/restball_rerank.py`](../src/predicate_rerank/restball_rerank.py)):
re-rank of the DirectionFusion top-200 with
`S(x) = Σ_c log P(x satisfies c) + log P(#mismatch on the rest ≤ 2) [+ λ·cos]`,
posteriors from 40 logistic probes (train only, fixed budget of 400 steps, same
recipe as I1; validation AUC: mean 0.921, min Oval_Face 0.731). The
Poisson-binomial tail (3-state DP) is an order semantics that no dot product
can express, and on its own it discounts the weak probes (agreement→0.5 ⇒ no
discrimination, no harm) — this is what the z-score sums lack.
